Privacy Policy for Clap to Find Phone: Siren Pro

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clap to Find Phone: Siren Pro ("we", "our", or "the application") handles user data and device hardware permissions. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring zero unnecessary data access.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Clap to Find Phone: Siren Pro is engineered with strict privacy principles:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device hardware IDs.
  • The application operates 100% locally on your device without requiring cloud accounts or remote servers.

2. Permissions & Hardware Usage

To provide its core functionality, the app requests the following Android runtime permissions:

A. Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)

Required strictly to detect acoustic transient spikes (such as hand clapping). Audio frames are sampled in volatile device RAM in real-time. Audio is NEVER recorded, NEVER saved to storage, and NEVER transmitted over the internet. Raw frames are immediately discarded milliseconds after amplitude evaluation.

B. Camera LED Flash (`android.permission.CAMERA`)

Required solely to activate the device's camera LED flash (flashlight strobe) during an alert. The camera sensor is never turned on to capture pictures or videos.

C. Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)

Permits the phone's internal vibration motor to pulse during an active alarm alert.

D. Foreground Service & Notifications

Per Android 14+ background execution guidelines, an ongoing notification remains visible in the system status shade while acoustic monitoring is active, providing full transparency.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties. The app contains no third-party tracking cookies or advertising tracking SDKs.
